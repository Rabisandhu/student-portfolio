How to Join a Student Club

Joining a club is the easiest way to make friends, learn new skills and be part of campus life. Follow these simple steps to get started.

Registration Steps

  1. Explore the clubs

    Visit the Club Categories page and shortlist 1–2 clubs that match your interests.

  2. Fill the registration form

    Fill the online club form with your name, roll number, department and semester, or register at the club stall during recruitment week.

  3. Attend the orientation

    Join the orientation session to meet the executive council and learn about the club's plans for the semester.

  4. Get your membership confirmed

    You will receive a confirmation email and an invite to the club's WhatsApp group within 3 working days.

Participation Guidelines

  • Attend at least 70% of club meetings each semester.
  • Respect all members and follow the university code of conduct.
  • Inform the club secretary if you cannot attend an event.
  • Students can be active members of at most two clubs at a time.
