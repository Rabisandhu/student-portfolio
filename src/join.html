How to Join a Student Club

A step-by-step guide for new students looking to become active society members.

Step 1: Choose Your Club

Browse our club categories and select an organization that aligns with your passions and goals.

Step 2: Submit Registration

Fill out the official society recruitment form during campus recruitment drives or online.

Step 3: Attend Orientation

Participate in the introductory session to meet the executive council and learn about upcoming projects.
